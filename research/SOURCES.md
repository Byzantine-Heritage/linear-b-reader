## Argos itself

Jonathan M. Hall, “Argos”, in Paul Cartledge and Paul Christesen (eds), *The Oxford History of the Archaic Greek World*, vol. I (2024), Oxford University Press, p. 15. The author explicitly states that no Late Bronze Age palace, tholos tomb or Linear B tablets had been found at Argos. Only the tablet finding is paraphrased on the site; it is dated to the survey and not conflated with the wider Argolid.
